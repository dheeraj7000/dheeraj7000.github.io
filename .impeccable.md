# Design Context

## Users
Primarily recruiters and hiring managers doing a fast credibility scan (role fit, standout projects, easy contact) — the page must front-load signal and stay scannable. A secondary audience of technical peers/researchers may read project descriptions and publications closely, so depth is preserved below the fold rather than cut.

## Brand Personality
Precise, technical, and research-lab/futuristic, but calm and understated rather than loud — the feel of a well-instrumented systems dashboard or lab telemetry readout, not cyberpunk neon. Confidence comes from restraint and exactness, not decoration.

## Aesthetic Direction
- Theme: dark (matches the technical/research-lab personality and the always-on "systems" feel).
- Accent: a single considered blue (OKLCH, hue ~241), used sparingly per the 60/30/10 rule — as text links, active states, and one status indicator — never as glow on every card/border.
- Typography: Archivo (display + body) paired with Martian Mono (labels, tags, index numbers, metadata) — avoids overused "AI portfolio" fonts (Inter, Space Grotesk/Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex, Instrument *).
- Layout: manifest/index style rather than bordered card grids — hairline dividers, numbered entries, a sticky section-index rail (desktop: left column; mobile: horizontal top bar) with scroll-spy.
- Explicitly avoided: gradient text, colored left-border accent stripes, glassmorphism, glow-on-hover cards, generic rounded-card-with-icon grids.

## Design Principles
1. Blue is a signal, not a decoration — reserve it for links, active/hover states, and the availability dot; never apply it as ambient glow.
2. Structure content as a precise index/manifest (numbered entries, hairline rules) rather than repeated icon+card blocks.
3. Recruiters skim, researchers read — keep hero/links front-loaded and scannable, but let project/publication entries carry real technical detail.
4. Motion is restrained: one staggered entrance on load, scroll-spy state changes — no decorative hover animation beyond arrow/color shifts.
5. Typography pairing (Archivo + Martian Mono) and the OKLCH blue-tinted neutral palette should carry forward into any future pages on this site for consistency.
