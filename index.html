About

I build agentic AI systems and the infrastructure that keeps them honest — graph neural networks, LLM agent architectures, multi-agent orchestration, evaluation harnesses, and AI security. Most of my work sits at the seam between a model and a production system: policy layers, provenance, trust scoring, and evaluation gates.

Currently an M.S. Computer Science candidate at Arizona State University (Dec 2026), with papers on arXiv and ResearchGate and merged security contributions to open-source agent infrastructure.
